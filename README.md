# Cut Tracker

Build a fitness and diet tracking app called Cut Tracker. For now, just set up:

1. A homepage with the title "Cut and Buk Tracker" and a placeholder "Connect Whoop" button (doesn't need to work yet).

2. A route at /whoop-callback that just shows "Whoop connected successfully" as placeholder text — this will later handle the OAuth login return.

3. A route at /privacy with a simple privacy policy page. Use this text: "Cut Tracker is a personal fitness tracking tool. It stores your Whoop fitness data (recovery, sleep, strain, workouts, body measurements) and your manually logged food and weight entries to provide personalized diet recommendations. Data is used only within this app and is not shared with third parties. Contact: rohit.16dec@gmail.com"

Keep it simple — no styling needed yet, this is a skeleton to get a live URL.
